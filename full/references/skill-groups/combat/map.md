# Combat map

The encounter owns a temporary five-foot-grid combat map. Patch it only through
`combat_map_patch` and only from reviewed scene/map evidence or an explicit
bounded DM spatial ruling.

Keep public and DM-only layers separate. Walls, blocking, difficult terrain,
occupancy, elevation, cover, hazards, and actor placement must retain source or
ruling provenance. A decorative image is not mechanical geometry.

Map revision participates in movement and reaction validation. Re-query the map
after any patch, join, restore, or movement conflict.
